Food for All Initiative

Our Mission

Food for All Initiative is a Nairobi-based NGO dedicated to fighting hunger and food insecurity. Our mission is to provide food to vulnerable families, support communities and promote access to nutritious meals for everyone.

Our Programmes

  1. Food Donation Programme – Providing food to needy families.
  2. School Feeding Programme – Supplying meals to children in schools.
  3. Community Farming Programme – Teaching communities how to grow their own food.
